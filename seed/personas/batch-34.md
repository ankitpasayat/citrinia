# Batch 34 — Multilingual life and code-switching

This batch is daily-life multilingualism — learning a language, living in translation, and the policy decisions that decide which language a form, a dub or a classroom defaults to.
India-centred personas: 2 of 6. Target: this batch's peels should be about 35% about India.
